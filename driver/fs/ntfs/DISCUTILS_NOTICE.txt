DiscUtils NTFS journal layout and replay reference
Repository: https://github.com/LTRData/DiscUtils
Commit: b8c77f834ad0c43baf667b1fa7a8ff42c9e360b8

The native journal.cpp/journal.h use reviewed layout and replay conventions.
recovery.cpp/recovery.h and recovery_actions.cpp adapt checkpoint analysis,
LFS record/tail/ring reading, redo/undo and checked logical FILE/INDX operations
from the pinned MIT journal implementation. C++ caller-owned storage, limits,
validation and durable snapshot publication are local changes. No C# runtime
or archive is linked. Exact upstream source paths/hashes are in REFERENCES.json.
